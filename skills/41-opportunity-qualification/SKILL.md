# Opportunity qualification

> Qualification is deciding where to spend scarce selling time, not forcing a customer through a framework.

Use the existing founder-led four as the minimum:
- champion
- buyer
- pain with cost
- trigger

For repeatable enterprise sales, add:

- problem severity
- business consequence
- compelling event
- technical fit
- decision criteria
- decision process
- economic buyer
- champion strength
- competition/status quo
- procurement/security path
- next step/date

## Qualification levels

### Conversation
Interesting, but not enough evidence.

### Qualified opportunity
Must have:
- credible problem
- plausible business consequence
- identified trigger/timing
- product relevance
- next step

### Forecastable opportunity
Additionally:
- champion
- buyer/economic process
- decision process
- evaluation/business-case path
- target close event/date
- mutual next steps

## Disqualification

Make it easy to disqualify:
- no meaningful pain
- no urgency
- no fit
- no access
- no economic path
- no reason to change
- timing outside horizon

"Not now" should not be hidden in pipeline.
