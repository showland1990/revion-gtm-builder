# Talk to users

Interview relevant users and buyers without pitching.

Explore:
- magic-wand change
- stakes and consequence
- why now
- origin of the problem
- alternatives tried
- cost of doing nothing
- trusted information sources

Mine exact customer language into pains, gains, jobs and environmental changes. Tag findings `[validated]`, `[observed]`, `[inferred]`, or `[assumption]`.

Feed validated evidence back into ICP, positioning, value, pricing and roadmap.
