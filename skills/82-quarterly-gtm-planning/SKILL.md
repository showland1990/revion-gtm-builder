# Quarterly GTM planning

> Quarterly planning should narrow focus, not create an exhaustive wish list.

## Inputs

- annual plan
- last quarter performance
- current GTM constraint
- funnel math
- capacity
- product roadmap
- market changes
- customer evidence

## Choose

- company GTM objective
- primary constraint
- 3 to 5 cross-functional priorities
- targets
- leading indicators
- key experiments
- dependencies
- parked work

## Planning test

Every priority must answer:
- what problem?
- why now?
- owner?
- metric?
- baseline?
- target?
- dependency?
- decision unlocked?

## Output

Update:
- `gtm-roadmap.md`
- `revenue-plan.md`
- quarterly operating plan
