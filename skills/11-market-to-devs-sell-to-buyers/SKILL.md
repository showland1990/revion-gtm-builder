# Market to practitioners, sell to buyers

Separate adopter, champion, technical evaluator, economic buyer and procurement/security.

Translate practitioner value into business value. Equip the champion with the business case, consequence, why-now, proof and trust material.

For many developer products the right model is PLG plus sales assist where a human materially helps.
