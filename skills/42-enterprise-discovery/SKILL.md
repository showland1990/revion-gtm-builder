# Enterprise discovery

> Discovery is not a checklist. It is a joint diagnosis of whether a material problem exists, why it matters, and whether change is worth the effort.

## Discovery layers

### Context
- what are they trying to achieve?
- what changed?
- current environment/workflow

### Problem
- where does the current approach break?
- who feels it?
- frequency/severity

### Consequence
- time
- money
- risk
- delay
- reliability
- opportunity cost
- executive impact

### Current alternative
- workaround
- incumbent
- internal build
- do nothing
- why tolerated

### Trigger
- why now?
- what deadline/event matters?
- what happens if they wait?

### Desired outcome
- technical success
- team success
- business success
- executive success

### Buying process
- who evaluates?
- who approves?
- who can veto?
- how is budget released?
- security/procurement path?

## Quantification

Use customer inputs, not invented ROI.

Ask:
- volume
- frequency
- people affected
- time per event
- loaded cost if customer can estimate
- incident/risk exposure
- revenue/release impact

## Discovery output

Record:
- problem
- evidence
- exact customer language
- quantified consequence
- trigger
- desired outcomes
- stakeholders
- open questions
- next step/date

Update the opportunity file immediately.
