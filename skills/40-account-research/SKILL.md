# Account research

> Research exists to improve commercial judgment, not to produce a biography of the prospect.

For each account answer:

## Company
- what they do
- size/stage
- relevant business priorities
- recent strategic changes
- geography / regulatory context

## Technical / operating context
- likely stack
- team topology
- integrations
- scale
- maturity
- current alternative if inferable

## Trigger
- what changed
- why it may create urgency
- confidence
- source

## Problem hypothesis
- likely pain
- affected team
- business consequence
- what remains assumption

## Buying committee hypothesis
- likely adopter
- champion candidate
- technical evaluator
- economic buyer
- executive sponsor
- security/procurement

## Competitive context
- incumbent
- internal build
- do nothing
- competitor

## Proof selection
Choose only proof relevant to:
- same segment
- same problem
- same scale
- same technical environment

## Output

Write a short account brief, not a giant dossier.

Every inferred claim should be labeled as hypothesis until validated.
