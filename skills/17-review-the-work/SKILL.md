# Review the work

Review as a skeptical operator.

Check ICP specificity, evidence, customer language, consistency, differentiation, proof, unsupported claims, motion fit, CTA, measurement and likely objections.

Return what is strong, weak, contradictory, risky and exactly what to improve. Do not reward polish when the strategic premise is weak.
