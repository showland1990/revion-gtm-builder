# Unit economics

> Growth quality depends on what it costs to acquire, serve and retain revenue, not only how fast topline grows.

## Core metrics

- CAC
- CAC payback
- gross margin
- logo retention
- GRR
- NRR
- expansion rate
- churn
- LTV
- contribution margin
- sales efficiency

## CAC

Define inclusion policy:
- marketing spend
- sales comp
- tools
- agencies
- event spend
- allocated GTM overhead

Separate:
- blended CAC
- new-logo CAC
- PLG/self-serve CAC
- sales-led CAC
- paid-channel CAC

## Payback

Use gross-margin-adjusted contribution where possible.

## LTV

Avoid simplistic infinite formulas.
Use actual retention cohorts when enough history exists.

## Segment economics

Compare:
- segment
- source
- ACV band
- motion
- geography
- product plan

A high CAC can still be good if retention/expansion justify it.
