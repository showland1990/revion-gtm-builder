# Evaluation and demo

> A demo proves relevance. An evaluation proves the customer can safely get the outcome in their environment.

## Demo

Before demoing, know:
- use case
- pain
- audience
- desired outcome
- objections/risks

Structure:
1. restate their problem
2. show the shortest path to their desired outcome
3. prove relevant differentiation
4. address role-specific risk
5. confirm relevance
6. agree next validation step

Do not feature-tour.

## Evaluation / pilot

Never begin an open-ended POC.

Agree in advance:
- business objective
- technical use case
- participants
- scope
- environment/data
- success criteria
- baseline
- measurement
- responsibilities
- duration
- decision at the end

## Evaluation success criteria

Must be:
- observable
- relevant to purchase
- agreed by customer
- achievable within evaluation
- connected to buyer outcome

## Failure modes

- free consulting
- no decision owner
- no timeline
- no agreed success criteria
- testing edge cases unrelated to purchase
- pilot ends with "we'll discuss internally"

## Output

Write evaluation plan into opportunity file and, when appropriate, a mutual action plan.
