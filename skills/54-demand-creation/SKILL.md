# Demand creation

> Demand creation makes the right people care before they are actively shopping.

## Preconditions

Read:
- ICP
- problem/triggers
- category narrative
- messaging house
- proof

If the company cannot explain the problem and why now, do not solve it with more content volume.

## Build the demand thesis

Answer:
1. What does the market believe today?
2. What important thing are they underestimating or misunderstanding?
3. What changed?
4. Why is the status quo increasingly costly?
5. What should they believe instead?
6. What proof makes the new belief credible?
7. What category/approach becomes logical after they accept that belief?

## Narrative themes

Choose 3 to 5 durable themes:
- market change
- painful problem
- flawed old way
- new operating model
- proof/results
- practitioner education

Every campaign/content piece should connect to a theme.

## Create memory, not only clicks

Demand creation needs repeated credible exposure:
- founder/expert content
- technical education
- community
- social
- research/benchmarks
- podcasts/events
- partners
- customer stories
- paid amplification

Measure:
- target audience reach
- branded/direct/search lift
- engaged target accounts
- high-quality subscriptions/community
- self-reported attribution
- downstream activated users/opportunities
- influenced pipeline with explicit confidence

Do not use impressions alone as success.
