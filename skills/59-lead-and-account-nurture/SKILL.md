# Lead and account nurture

> Nurture should reduce uncertainty and keep relevance alive until timing catches up.

## Segment by state

Possible states:
- engaged but not ready
- high-fit unactivated
- activated but not PQL
- PQL not sales-ready
- closed-lost timing
- no-decision
- target account with weak trigger
- event attendee
- content subscriber
- dormant champion

## For each state define

- why they are not progressing
- what would change their mind/timing
- content/proof needed
- trigger to watch
- cadence
- channel
- exit condition
- sales suppression
- measurement

## Nurture content

Use:
- new proof
- relevant educational insight
- benchmark/research
- use-case example
- integration/update tied to their blocker
- event/trigger
- customer story
- category POV

Do not send "just checking in."

## Account-level nurture

For complex B2B, nurture can target multiple buying roles:
- practitioner
- manager
- executive
- security

Keep messaging role-specific but strategically consistent.
