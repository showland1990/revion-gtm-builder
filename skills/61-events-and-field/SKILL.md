# Events and field

> An event is valuable when it creates concentrated trust, learning, relationships or deal progression.

## Choose the job

An event can:
- create category demand
- educate practitioners
- gather community
- generate target-account meetings
- accelerate open opportunities
- create customer proof
- enable partners
- support expansion

Pick the primary job.

## Event types

- owned webinar/workshop
- technical roundtable
- customer panel
- executive dinner
- conference sponsorship
- speaking
- community meetup
- virtual summit
- hands-on lab

## Plan

Define:
- audience/accounts
- reason to attend
- content/value
- speakers
- buying roles
- sales/customer follow-up
- product path
- content reuse
- success metrics

## Conference rule

Do not measure badge scans as pipeline.
Separate:
- meaningful target-account meetings
- product trials
- qualified follow-ups
- open-deal acceleration
- influenced revenue

## Output

Add event portfolio and playbook to revenue marketing model.
