# Pricing

Decide in order:
1. Value metric
2. Packaging
3. Price

A good value metric grows with value, is understandable and reasonably predictable.

PLG: give individual/core value; charge for team, scale, automation or advanced workflow. Enterprise can capture trust, governance, security, deployment and procurement needs.

Use customer evidence and value anchoring, not infrastructure cost or fear. Avoid surprise pricing.
