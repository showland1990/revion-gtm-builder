# Messaging house

> Channels should not invent their own positioning. One commercial truth should render differently by audience and stage.

Read `docs/gtm-cofounder/gtm-context.md`.

Do not create messaging until ICP, pain, trigger, value, alternatives and proof are at least explicit hypotheses.

## Build from evidence

### 1. Market change
What changed that makes the problem important now?

### 2. Old way
What do customers do today?

### 3. Failure
Why does the old way break under the new reality?

### 4. Stakes
What does that cost?

### 5. Better future
What outcome becomes possible?

### 6. Product role
How does the product enable that future?

### 7. Differentiation
Why this approach rather than the alternatives?

### 8. Proof
Why should anyone believe the claim?

## Produce

### Category line
A short answer to "what is it?"

### One-liner
For the primary user, in plain language.

### 30-second explanation
Problem -> outcome -> difference -> proof.

### Positioning statement
For [ICP] who [problem/trigger], [product] is [category] that [outcome]. Unlike [alternative], [differentiation], proven by [proof].

### Persona matrix
For every buying role:
- pain
- desired outcome
- message
- proof
- objection
- CTA

### Claim ledger
Every strong claim must point to evidence.
If evidence is weak, soften the wording.

### Language bank
Preserve exact customer phrases.

## Channel rendering

The messaging house is canonical. These are renderings:

- homepage: user problem + aha + proof + try
- lifecycle: next behavior + value
- outbound: account trigger + persona pain + proof
- discovery: hypotheses, not pitch
- sales deck: organizational stakes + proof + business case
- executive: strategic/economic outcome
- security: risk/control proof

Update `gtm-context.md`; do not fork separate truths.
