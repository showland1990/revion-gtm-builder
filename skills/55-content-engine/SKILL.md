# Content engine

> Content should answer the market's real questions and strengthen memory, trust and buying confidence.

Use `founder-led-content` for founder voice and problem-space authority. This skill builds the scalable operating system around it.

## Content portfolio

Balance:

### POV / category
Changes beliefs.

### Education
Helps practitioners solve the problem.

### Use-case
Shows when/how the product creates value.

### Proof
Customer outcomes, benchmarks, examples.

### Buyer enablement
ROI, security, implementation, comparison, stakeholder concerns.

### Product education
Helps activation and adoption.

## Build a problem-to-content map

For each ICP/persona/state capture:
- question
- pain
- search/community language
- content asset
- proof
- CTA
- reuse/distribution plan

## Content atomization

One strong source can become:
- deep article
- technical guide
- short posts
- sales snippet
- lifecycle message
- webinar/talk
- comparison section
- enablement asset

Do not mechanically repurpose weak content.

## Editorial standards

Every piece should have at least one:
- original insight
- proprietary data
- real example
- concrete tutorial
- customer proof
- strong, defensible point of view

Avoid AI-generated commodity volume.

## Distribution

Content is unfinished without a distribution plan:
- owned
- founder/team
- community
- partners
- search
- paid amplification
- sales/CS reuse

## Measurement

Track by intent:
- reach/memory for POV
- qualified traffic for education
- activation for product education
- opportunity influence for buyer content
- conversion for high-intent content
