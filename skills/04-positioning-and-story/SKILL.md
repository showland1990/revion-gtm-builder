# Positioning and story

The customer is the hero. The product is the enabling mechanism.

Build:
1. Market change
2. Painful problem
3. Why the old way fails
4. Stakes / why now
5. Better future
6. Product role
7. Differentiation
8. Proof

Prefer problem-level differentiation over feature matrices. If the problem/language is not validated, route to `talk-to-users`.
