# Expansion and virality

> Expansion is not only upsell. It is the product becoming useful to more of the customer's organization.

## Expansion vectors

Identify which are real:
- seats/users
- teams/workspaces
- projects/workloads
- usage/consumption
- features
- integrations
- environments
- regions
- business units
- governance/security tier

For each capture behavior indicating readiness, user who experiences the constraint, beneficiary, buyer, upgrade path, product intervention and human intervention.

## Product distribution loops

A real loop has:
1. user receives value
2. normal product use creates an artifact/invite/exposure
3. another relevant person sees value
4. they enter the product
5. they can create more exposure

Examples: collaboration invite, shared dashboard/report, public artifact, embedded output, generated badge/link, workflow handoff.

Do not bolt on referrals if sharing is unnatural.

## Team activation

Define when an account becomes a team, not just a collection of users.
Potential behaviors include multiple active users, shared project, permission setup, shared integration, collaborative workflow or governance configuration.

Measure retention and monetization differences between single-user and team-activated accounts.

## Output

Update `plg-model.md` with expansion vectors, expansion-ready signals, product loops, team activation and account expansion paths.
