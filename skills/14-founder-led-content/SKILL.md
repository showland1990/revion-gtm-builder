# Founder-led content

Teach the problem space and own a point of view.

Use market/problem thesis, technical education, tutorials, real examples, customer evidence and transparent learning. For developer products, treat README/docs/repo metadata as distribution surfaces.

Avoid changelog-as-content and generic AI-generated volume.
