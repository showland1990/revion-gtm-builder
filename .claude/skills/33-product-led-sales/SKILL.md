# Product-led sales

> Sales-assist should feel like useful help arriving at the moment an account becomes organizationally complex, not a rep pouncing on a signup.

Read `gtm-context.md`, `plg-model.md`, `pql-model.md` and buying committee.

## Decide why a human helps

Valid reasons:
- implementation complexity
- multi-team rollout
- security/procurement
- business case
- pricing/packaging complexity
- enterprise deployment
- strategic expansion
- executive alignment

If no human value exists, keep it self-serve.

## Routing

For each qualified account specify account tier, signal/reason code, owner, SLA, contact strategy and suppression conditions.

## Outreach context

Every outreach should answer:
- What did we observe?
- Why might it matter?
- What help can we offer?
- Why is this relevant to their role?

Do not expose creepy detail. Use product context at a level that feels expected and helpful.

## Sequence by relationship

### Existing active champion
Reference real use and ask about rollout.

### Active account, no known champion
Find the likely owner before escalating.

### Enterprise-intent action
Respond quickly with relevant technical/security/commercial help.

### High fit but weak value
Do not push sales. Fix activation or nurture.

## Product-led discovery

Start from observed behavior, then validate use case, problem, scale, organizational impact, trigger, stakeholders and desired rollout.
Do not assume usage equals pain or authority.

## Feedback loop

Sales returns structured reasons: false-positive PQL, wrong persona, no pain, no urgency, no budget, timing, missing feature, competitor, successful expansion.
Use outcomes to recalibrate PQL.

## Output

Update `pql-model.md` and roadmap with routing, SLA, sales-assist plays, feedback fields and handback rules.
