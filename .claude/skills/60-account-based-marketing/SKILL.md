# Account-based marketing

> ABM is coordinated pursuit of a valuable account, not putting its logo on an ad.

## ABM gate

Use ABM only when:
- target account universe is finite enough
- ACV/LTV justifies effort
- buying committee complexity matters
- account-level signals exist
- sales capacity can act
- differentiated proof/message exists

If not, use segment-based demand generation instead.

## ABM tiers

### 1:1
Very high-value account, custom plan.

### 1:few
Small cluster sharing industry/use case/trigger.

### 1:many
Scaled account targeting with real segmentation, not cosmetic personalization.

## Account plan

For each account:
- strategic reason
- trigger
- likely pain
- opportunity hypothesis
- buying committee
- existing relationships/usage
- content/proof
- gaps
- marketing actions
- sales actions
- executive/partner actions
- success criteria

## Engagement

Measure meaningful actions:
- target persona engagement
- product usage
- event attendance
- high-intent content
- stakeholder breadth
- sales conversation
- opportunity progression

Avoid opaque "account engagement score" without explainable events.
