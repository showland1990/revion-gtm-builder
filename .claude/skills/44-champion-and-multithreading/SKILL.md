# Champion and multithreading

> A friendly user is not necessarily a champion. A real champion actively spends credibility to make the purchase happen.

## Champion score

Assess 0-2 each:
- pain/need
- influence
- access to power
- internal credibility
- shares information
- drives next steps
- understands value
- compelling event

Classify:
- weak
- developing
- strong

## Champion tests

Ask them to:
- explain internal process
- introduce a stakeholder
- validate business case
- co-author success criteria
- schedule next step
- share decision timeline

A champion who never acts may be a coach or user, not a champion.

## Multithreading map

At minimum map:
- user/adopter
- champion
- technical evaluator
- manager
- economic buyer
- executive sponsor
- security/legal/procurement

For each:
- influence
- stance
- pain
- desired outcome
- required proof
- relationship owner
- next action

## Anti-single-thread rule

Late-stage enterprise opportunity with one contact = high risk.

Do not go around a champion secretly; earn introductions and explain why broader alignment helps the project.

## Output

Update stakeholder map and champion score in opportunity file.
