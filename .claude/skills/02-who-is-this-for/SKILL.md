# Who is this for?

If the ICP does not exclude anyone, it is not an ICP.

Define:
1. Company shape
2. Technical context
3. Trigger
4. Pain in the customer's words
5. Who is deliberately out

Separate discoverer/adopter, evaluator, champion and economic buyer. Use JTBD: `When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome].`

Do not use TAM as targeting or personas as job titles only. If pain is not grounded in customer language, route to `talk-to-users`.

Update founder brief or GTM context with ICP, anti-ICP, roles and JTBD.
