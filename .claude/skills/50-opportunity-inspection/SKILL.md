# Opportunity inspection

> Inspection is not rep interrogation. It is a structured search for uncertainty that can still be resolved.

Read the opportunity file.

## Inspect

Score Red / Amber / Green:
- ICP fit
- problem
- quantified consequence
- trigger
- champion
- economic buyer
- decision criteria
- decision process
- technical evaluation
- competition/status quo
- business case
- security/legal
- procurement
- multithreading
- next step
- close-date credibility

## Questions

- What evidence supports this stage?
- What would have to be true for the customer to sign?
- Which of those things is not yet true?
- Who can still kill the deal?
- Why would they do nothing?
- Why this quarter?
- Is the next step customer-owned?
- Is the close date linked to customer reality?

## Output

Produce:
- current stage
- forecast category recommendation
- top three risks
- missing evidence
- single highest-leverage next action
- whether to advance, hold, regress or disqualify

Update opportunity file.
