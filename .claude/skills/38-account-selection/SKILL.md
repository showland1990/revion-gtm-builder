# Account selection

> Account selection is where sales efficiency starts. Poor targeting cannot be rescued by better copy.

Read `gtm-context.md`, segmentation, and PQL model if available.

## Build account criteria

Separate:

### Fit
- company size
- team size
- industry
- geography
- technical environment
- architecture
- maturity
- budget potential
- compliance/security profile

### Need
- known pain pattern
- relevant use case
- current alternative
- structural reason they should care

### Timing / trigger
- migration
- hiring
- funding
- incident
- launch
- regulation
- platform change
- usage growth
- contract renewal
- product signal

### Strategic value
- ACV potential
- expansion
- logo value
- market adjacency
- partner/ecosystem relevance

### Reachability
- known champion
- product users
- community relationship
- accessible buying committee
- credible proof

## Tiering

Example:
- Tier 1: high fit + high trigger + high value, deep research/human attention
- Tier 2: strong fit + moderate signal, scaled personalization
- Tier 3: fit but no current trigger, nurture/monitor

Do not tier solely by employee count.

## Disqualifiers

Explicitly define:
- anti-ICP
- incompatible stack
- unsupported geography
- uneconomic ACV
- procurement burden disproportionate to value
- no credible use case
- churn-prone profile

## Output

Write/update target-account section in `sales-model.md`:
- scoring logic
- tiers
- trigger library
- disqualifiers
- account research requirements
- ownership and review cadence
