# Revenue marketing operating review

> Review whether marketing is improving the commercial system, not whether the team shipped enough campaigns.

Read:
- revenue marketing model
- PLG model
- sales scorecard
- attribution model
- roadmap

## Scorecard

Review:
- qualified audience/traffic
- activated users from marketing
- PQL/PQA from marketing
- inbound qualified conversations
- opportunities
- pipeline created
- pipeline influenced with confidence
- win rate by source
- CAC/payback where meaningful
- target-account progression
- content/theme performance
- lifecycle progression
- customer advocacy
- expansion pipeline
- attribution confidence

## Diagnose

Ask:
1. Is the ICP/audience quality right?
2. Is demand creation increasing market pull?
3. Are high-intent journeys captured?
4. Are conversion paths working?
5. Are lifecycle interventions progressing state?
6. Are marketing-sourced accounts becoming good customers?
7. Which programs create incremental outcomes?
8. Where is marketing compensating for a product/sales problem?
9. What customer/sales learning should change messaging?

## Avoid vanity

Red flags:
- traffic up, activation down
- leads up, opportunity quality down
- MQLs up, pipeline flat
- influenced pipeline up because attribution rules broadened
- event scans up, meetings/opportunities flat
- content volume up, qualified engagement flat

## Output

Update:
- revenue marketing model
- attribution confidence
- GTM context evidence
- roadmap
