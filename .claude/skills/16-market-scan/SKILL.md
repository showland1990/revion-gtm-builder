# Market scan

Research current category definitions, competitors, substitutes/status quo, positioning, pricing/packaging, target segments and meaningful market changes.

Separate facts from inference. Compare changes against current GTM context and state what should actually alter strategy.

Do not produce competitor trivia without implications.
