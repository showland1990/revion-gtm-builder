# Customer marketing

> The customer base is both a revenue asset and a truth source. Do not treat customers as logo inventory.

## Customer lifecycle jobs

- adoption education
- value realization
- community
- executive communication
- proof creation
- advocacy
- references
- reviews
- events
- expansion support

## Proof mining

Identify customers with:
- strong outcome
- strategic logo
- distinctive use case
- quantified value
- technical depth
- category credibility

Capture:
- before
- trigger
- alternative
- implementation
- result
- quantified outcome
- customer words
- stakeholder perspectives

Get permission for public use.

## Advocacy ladder

Low friction:
- quote
- review
- logo

Medium:
- reference call
- webinar
- community contribution

High:
- case study
- conference
- analyst/reference program
- executive advocacy

Do not overuse the same advocate.

## Feedback loop

Customer evidence should update:
- GTM context
- messaging
- proof library
- sales enablement
- onboarding
- product roadmap hypotheses
