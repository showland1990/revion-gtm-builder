# Enterprise closing

> Closing is the consequence of enough customer conviction plus a navigable decision process.

## Pre-close inspection

Confirm:
- problem still matters
- compelling event still real
- champion active
- buyer aligned
- success criteria met
- competition/status quo understood
- business case accepted
- security complete
- legal path known
- procurement path known
- commercial terms aligned
- signature authority known
- next steps/dates explicit

## Red flags

- "verbal yes" but buyer never met
- procurement not started
- no compelling event
- champion stopped responding
- evaluation passed but business case absent
- target date is seller-created
- legal/security requirements unknown
- one-threaded relationship

## Commit criteria

Only call a deal commit when:
- customer has a defined decision path
- major blockers are known
- stakeholder alignment is evidenced
- close date maps to customer process/event
- mutual next steps are active

## Closed won handoff

Capture:
- promised outcomes
- scope
- stakeholders
- risks
- implementation commitments
- expansion hypothesis
- proof/case-study potential

## Closed lost

Capture structured reason:
- no decision
- timing
- budget
- competitor
- internal build
- product gap
- security/legal
- champion failure
- value/business case
- pricing
- other

Never use "lost to competitor" if the real outcome was no decision.

## Output

Update opportunity and pipeline learning.
