# Territory and capacity planning

> Capacity planning asks whether the team can create and convert enough qualified opportunity to hit the plan.

## Inputs

- revenue target
- segment mix
- ACV
- win rate
- sales cycle
- rep productivity
- ramp time
- attrition
- account universe
- marketing/PQL contribution
- partner contribution

## Rep capacity

Model:
- ramped productivity
- ramp schedule
- active pipeline load
- account load
- meeting/opportunity capacity

## Territory design

Use:
- opportunity potential
- segment
- geography
- product fit
- account density
- support burden
- language/regulatory context

Avoid purely equal account counts.

## Coverage risk

Flag:
- too few target accounts
- too much pipeline per rep
- too little pipeline creation
- overdependence on a few whales
- insufficient SDR/marketing/product contribution

## Output

Write:
`docs/gtm-cofounder/capacity-plan.md`
