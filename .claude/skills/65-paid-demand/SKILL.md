# Paid demand

> Paid should amplify something that already works or capture high-intent demand. It should not be used to hide weak positioning or broken conversion.

## Paid roles

### Capture
High-intent search/category/comparison.

### Amplify
Strong content/research/customer proof.

### Target
Reach known strategic accounts/personas.

### Retarget
Help relevant evaluators return, with restraint.

## Preconditions

Before scaling paid:
- ICP clear
- message has evidence
- landing path converts
- activation/qualification measurable
- attribution fields exist
- downstream economics observable

## Economics

Measure by stage:
- spend
- qualified visitor
- signup
- activation
- PQL
- opportunity
- pipeline
- customer
- CAC/payback

Use channel-specific leading indicators but optimize toward downstream economics.

## Experimentation

Test one variable where possible:
- audience
- intent
- message
- creative
- offer
- landing experience

## Guardrails

Avoid:
- misleading ads
- dark-pattern retargeting
- broad spend before fit
- measuring only CTR/CPL
- importing low-quality "leads" to satisfy volume targets

## Output

Write paid portfolio and economics into revenue marketing model.
