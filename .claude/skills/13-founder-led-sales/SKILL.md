# Founder-led sales

Every real early deal needs champion, buyer, pain with cost and trigger.

Start with relevant/engaged accounts. Run discovery before pitch. Demo their use case. Translate practitioner value to business value. Arm the champion. Leave every call with a next step/date. Multithread before late stage.

Do not outsource a motion the founder has not learned.
