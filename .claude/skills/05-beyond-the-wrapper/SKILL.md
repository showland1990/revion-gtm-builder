---

# Beyond the wrapper

Stress-test defensibility across proprietary workflow, data/feedback loops, integrations, reliability, distribution, switching costs, collaboration/network effects, domain expertise, trust/governance and accumulated customer context.

Separate current differentiation from future defensibility. Never invent a moat.

Output the strongest defensibility thesis, gaps and evidence to build next.
