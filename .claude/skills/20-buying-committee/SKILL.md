# Buying committee

> Adoption is a product journey. Purchase is an organizational journey. In complex B2B, both must work.

Read `docs/gtm-cofounder/gtm-context.md`.

## Map roles, not titles

Required roles may include:
- discoverer
- adopter / user
- champion
- technical evaluator
- manager
- economic buyer
- executive sponsor
- security
- legal
- procurement
- finance
- blocker

The same person can hold multiple roles in small companies.

## For each role answer

- What are they trying to accomplish?
- What do they personally gain?
- What do they fear?
- What evidence do they need?
- What objection will they raise?
- When do they enter?
- How much influence do they have?
- What message is relevant?
- What CTA is appropriate?

## Champion test

A real champion:
- has meaningful pain
- has influence or access
- shares internal context
- helps navigate stakeholders
- actively advances next steps
- can articulate value internally
- has a compelling reason to act

Do not call a friendly user a champion automatically.

## Buying-path output

Create the likely sequence:

```text
discover -> try -> activate -> internal advocate -> technical validation
-> business case -> executive/budget approval -> security/legal -> procurement -> close
```

Delete stages that do not apply.

Update `gtm-context.md` with the role map and missing evidence.
