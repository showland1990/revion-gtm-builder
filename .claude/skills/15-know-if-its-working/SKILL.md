# Know if it is working

Retention is the first gate. Use cohorts.

Track honest metrics for first value/activation, retained behavior, acquisition quality, paid/PQL conversion and expansion where relevant.

Use self-reported attribution alongside tracked data. Do not let stars, pageviews, impressions or raw signup volume masquerade as GTM success.
