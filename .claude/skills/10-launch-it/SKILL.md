# Launch it

A launch amplifies a message and product experience that already work.

Check audience/problem, positioning, activation path, proof, target channels, CTA and follow-up.

Build around the customer's problem/use case/evidence, not vendor excitement. Measure activated users, relevant conversations and retained demand, not attention alone.
