# Negotiation and commercials

> Never give something away without understanding what you receive in return.

## Prepare

Know:
- value/business case
- target price
- floor/guardrails
- term preference
- payment terms
- scope
- implementation/support
- legal/security concessions
- alternatives/BATNA
- approval authority

## Trade, do not concede

Potential trades:
- discount ↔ longer term
- discount ↔ upfront payment
- discount ↔ reduced scope
- discount ↔ standard terms
- discount ↔ reference/case study only when genuinely appropriate and agreed
- flexibility ↔ faster signature

Avoid artificial deadlines or deceptive scarcity.

## Separate issues

Negotiate independently:
- price
- volume
- term
- payment
- SLA/support
- implementation
- legal risk
- data/security
- renewal
- termination

Bundling everything creates confusion.

## Procurement

Expect procurement to optimize price even after business approval.
Return to:
- agreed value
- agreed scope
- market/alternative logic
- reciprocal trades

## Output

Record negotiated positions, approvals and red lines in opportunity file.
