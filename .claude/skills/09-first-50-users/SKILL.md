# First 50 users

Find users where the problem already exists: relevant communities, GitHub/ecosystems, direct outreach to people visibly experiencing the problem, partner audiences and practitioner content.

Run small channel tests. Judge them by activated/retained users and learning, not traffic.

Do not scale spend until a channel produces relevant users and the product retains them.
